A · 01

What I am designing for, and the tradeoffs

The blueprint's north-star metric is Weekly Meaningful Seeds — seeds finished with at least one real cognitive interaction. That single choice decides the entire interface. A design optimised for session time would look like a feed. A design optimised for meaningful completion looks like a short, finite, closable reading surface with recall built into it.

So the product surface is deliberately small: one hero seed per day, four finite rails, no infinite scroll, and a player that shows one block at a time and lets you leave without a guilt dialog. Everything competitive — format variety, AI tutor, social — is out of round one because none of it moves WMS before content quality does.

Tradeoff · finite over infinite
Home ends. That costs sessions-per-day but protects the "not a feed" positioning and makes the Explore CTA meaningful rather than decorative.
Tradeoff · metadata over imagery
Seed cards lead with topic, promise, duration and reason instead of a photo. Less scroll-stopping, far better for slow networks, offline, and 200% text.
Tradeoff · weekly growth over daily streak
A 0–7 weekly ring with one grace day is less addictive than a midnight-reset streak. Accepted deliberately: the brief forbids shame mechanics, and a broken streak is the most common reason people uninstall this category.
THINGS I PUSHED BACK ON
«دانه کاشته شد» as the completion headline. Poetic, but ambiguous for a first-time user who needs to know the lesson is over. Metaphor moves to the visual (the growth ring) and the sub-line; headline stays literal: «تمام شد».
Sprout green #65A96B as progress fill. 1.88:1 against its track — invisible to a lot of people. Progress fills use brand primary; sprout is decorative only. Details in §04.
Sun accent as text. 1.88:1 on canvas. It becomes a fill-only token with an ink label, plus a separate darker accent.sunInk for type.
Garden as literal illustration. Plant art on every row makes five list states unreadable. The metaphor lives in one artifact — the concentric ring — reused at four sizes.
THE ONE-SENTENCE PROMISE

پنج دقیقه، یک ایده‌ی درست، با منبع مشخص — و هفته‌ی بعد هم یادت می‌ماند.

Five minutes, one correct idea, sourced — and you still remember it next week. Every screen in round one is judged against this line.

A · 02

Five design principles

01
فارسی، اصل است
Persian is the original
Every layout is composed right-to-left first. LTR is the port. Line height, نیم‌فاصله, Persian numerals and bidi isolation are correctness requirements, not polish.
02
یک بلوک، یک فکر
One block, one thought
The viewport holds one meaningful unit. Nothing peeks from below to bait a scroll. Density is the reader's choice, never the layout's trick.
03
منبع، بخشی از محتواست
The source is part of the content
Sources and a last-reviewed date sit one tap from every seed, and reporting an error is a normal action — not buried in settings.
04
آفلاین، حالت عادی است
Offline is a normal state
Cached, stale, queued and missing each have a designed appearance. No screen is allowed to be a spinner that never resolves.
05
رشد، نه فشار
Growth, not pressure
Progress is shown as accumulation, never as a loss you must prevent. No countdowns, no red streak, no shame copy anywhere in the string catalogue.

ادامه
Login before value
First launch is a form. Guest-first anonymous auth moves the account ask to after the first completion.
Everything centred
Centre-aligned Persian body text at 14–18px reads badly. New system: start-aligned, 17/30, line-height ≥ 1.65.
Colour-only answers
Green/red borders with no icon or text label. Fails WCAG 1.4.1. Replaced by icon + label + explanation.
No card metadata
Image + title only — no duration, difficulty, reason or offline state, so the user can't judge fit before tapping.

Correction 1 — progress fill
Sprout on a warm track measures 1.88:1, under the 3:1 floor for meaningful graphics. Light-mode progress fills use brand.primary (4.7:1 against the track). Dark mode keeps sprout — it measures 9.3:1 there.
Correction 2 — sun as type
Sun is a fill-only token in light mode. Where the reward/curiosity colour must be text, use the added accent.sunInk #8A6100 at 5.0:1. In dark mode the two collapse into one token.
Correction 4 — measure on the real parent
A token validated on bare canvas can still fail where it is actually used. Error red is 5.0:1 on canvas but 4.4:1 on its own 9% tint, and the pale neutral is 1.7:1 as an off-switch fill. Hence two added tokens: feedback.errorInk for text on tint and border.strong for state-carrying neutrals. Every pair ships with the background it was measured against.
Correction 3 — dark CTA label
White on #77B98A is only 2.1:1. Dark-mode primary buttons carry ink labels (8.2:1), which also reads as intentional rather than as a light-mode leftover.

PERSIAN TYPESETTING RULES
Line height ≥ 1.65 for anything body-sized. Persian descenders and dots collide below that.
نیم‌فاصله is data, not styling. ZWNJ (U+200C) lives in the string; never faked with a space. Search normalises it, display preserves it.
Persian punctuation: «…» not "…", ٬ ، ؛ ? — the Latin set is a content bug.
Never a Latin middot next to a digit. In Yekan Bakh, U+00B7 is glyph-identical to ۰ — «۶ دقیقه · مقدماتی» reads as «۶ دقیقه ۰ مقدماتی» and «۳۴ دقیقه» becomes ۳۴۰. Metadata separators are a drawn 4pt dot element in a flex row, never a text character, which also keeps the screen-reader string clean.
No underlines on Persian text. Links use weight + colour; focus uses a ring.
Bidi isolation: Latin names, URLs, formulas and version numbers get dir="ltr" + isolation, so «مقاله‌ی Olbers (1826) را ببینید» never reflows.
Numerals: Persian digits in prose and UI (۶ دقیقه); Latin digits only inside LTR-isolated technical strings.
200% DYNAMIC TYPE
Titles cap at 1.6× and switch to 3-line clamp; body scales fully. Buttons grow in height, never shrink their label. Answer options become full-width stacked blocks.

L · 08

Do not do

Each of these is a pattern this category defaults to, and each one is rejected for a stated reason. Sketched at small scale so the rejection is legible, not attractive.

هوشمندتر شو! 🚀
Purple gradient + rocket
Signals "startup app", not "credible source". It is also exactly what the current skeleton does. Editorial credibility comes from paper, ink and restraint.
۰
استریک از دست رفت!
۰۲:۱۴:۳۹ باقی مانده
Streak loss + countdown
Loss aversion works, and it is the top uninstall reason in this category. Weekly growth with one grace day keeps the habit without the punishment.
Colour-only right/wrong
Fails WCAG 1.4.1 and is unreadable for the ~8% of men with red-green deficiency. Every state carries an icon and a text label as well as colour.
Infinite feed
A bottomless list optimises time-in-app, the metric we explicitly rejected. Home ends after four finite rails and hands off to Explore.
کارت شیشه‌ای
Glass cards over imagery
Text contrast becomes a function of whatever photo loaded, which cannot be validated. Opaque card on flat canvas, always.
🌱🪴🌻
A plant on every row
Turns the metaphor into noise and makes five Garden states look identical. One artifact — the concentric ring — carries growth at four sizes.

WHAT COMES NEXT
Round 2 — screens. Every core flow, RTL first, light and dark: onboarding, Home, Explore, search, the eleven player blocks, feedback, completion, Garden, review, auth upgrade, offline, settings and the twelve system states.
Round 3 — prototype and handoff. Clickable first-run → first completion, offline resume and review; then the component library with the full state matrix, redlines at three widths, and React Native implementation notes.
DECIDED
Calendar: Jalali only. No switcher in round one. Dates appear in three places — the review date, the last-reviewed line on sources, and the Garden queue — all Jalali. Foreign source years carry an era marker («۱۹۸۷ م.») so a Gregorian publication date can never be misread as Jalali.
Imagery: commissioned illustration. Every image slot in the screens is a labelled placeholder with its intended aspect ratio and subject. No stock photography, which keeps card anatomy metadata-led and means alt text can be written at commission time rather than retrofitted.
Topic colour: six topics, three accent families. Sciences → primary, humanities → plum, practical → sun. Six distinct hues would collide with the correct / incorrect / offline semantics and leave no headroom for state colour.
